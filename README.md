## 📌 Featured Projects

### 🩺 [HealthAssist AI — Multi-Agent Healthcare Triage & Lab Analysis](https://github.com/lalit-mittal49/health-assistant-ai)
> **Stack:** Python · FastAPI · LangGraph · ChromaDB · React 18 · Vite · PyMuPDF · Tesseract OCR · SQLAlchemy

A full-stack intelligent health assistant web application combining deterministic clinical safety guardrails with multi-agent generative AI:
* **7-Agent LangGraph Orchestration:** Coordinates Symptom, Report, Knowledge (ChromaDB RAG), Orchestrator, Risk Scoring, Medication, and Diet agents.
* **Lab Report OCR & Biomarker Flagging:** Extracts and parses PDF/image lab reports via PyMuPDF and Tesseract OCR, flagging out-of-range biomarkers.
* **Medication Safety & Security:** Validates allergies and drug interactions, generates 7-day diet plans and clinical PDF reports (ReportLab), and protects patient data using AES Fernet encryption, bcrypt, and JWT authentication.

---

### 🍽️ [Restaurant Management & Predictive Analytics System](https://github.com/lalit-mittal49/Restaurant_management_project)
> **Stack:** Python · Streamlit · Scikit-Learn · Pandas · NumPy · Matplotlib · Seaborn

An interactive operational platform combining front-of-house ordering, inventory control, and ML forecasting:
* **Customer Ordering Portal:** Live menu ordering with automated stock validation and real-time ingredient ledger updates.
* **PIN-Authenticated Staff Dashboard:** Stock monitoring with automated `Danger Zone (< 10 units)` alerts and one-click restocking (`+10 units`).
* **Predictive Spend Analytics:** Linear Regression model forecasting customer spend (`Avg_Spend_Per_Visit_USD`) from dining behavior and loyalty features.
